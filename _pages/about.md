I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Stony Brook University, where I work with Professor R. Sekar on intrusion detection: developing an end-to-end detection pipeline and an efficient, information-rich Windows log producer.

Before Stony Brook, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science (2024) and an M.S. in Data Science (2025) at the University of Virginia. There I worked with Professor Wajih Ul Hassan on improving the throughput of intrusion detection systems, and on a capstone building computer vision software to digitize surgical flowsheets for developing countries (see my [publication](/publications/)).

I have also served as a teaching assistant for courses in systems and network administration, cybersecurity, machine learning, data science, and probability (see [teaching](/teaching/)), and I co-founded The Clover Project, a student-volunteer effort to reduce local food insecurity.
